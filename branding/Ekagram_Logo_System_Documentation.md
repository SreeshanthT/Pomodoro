# EKAGRAM
## Logo System & Brand Asset Documentation

**Dark-mode-first modular identity system**

---

## 1. Brand & Logo Concept

### Core idea

The Ekagram symbol is a modular geometric mark built from rounded tiles. The arrangement creates an abstract **E** through structure and negative space rather than drawing a conventional letter.

### Brand meanings

- **E** → Ekagram
- **Modules** → organized information and systems
- **Connected tiles** → community and communication
- **Grid** → digital platform and technology
- **Individual blocks** → people, ideas and conversations
- **Unified structure** → one ecosystem
- **Rounded geometry** → approachable, human technology

### Design principle

The negative spaces between the modules are part of the logo. The symbol should remain recognizable without relying on the wordmark.

---

## 2. Core Symbol Construction

The master symbol should be designed as a compact modular grid, approximately based on a **3 × 3 construction**. The preferred reference direction uses seven primary visual units with varied horizontal lengths.

### Construction characteristics

- Three upper modules
- Two middle modules
- Two lower modules
- One visually distinctive central/focal tile
- Consistent rounded corners and visual weight
- Controlled negative-space gaps
- No outlines, shadows or decorative effects

### Geometry guidelines

| Construction parameter | Recommended specification |
|---|---:|
| Master canvas | 1000 × 1000 units |
| Standard module | ≈ 150 × 150 units |
| Extended module | ≈ 300 × 150 units |
| Module gap | ≈ 20–30 units |
| Corner radius | ≈ 35–45 units |
| Safe area | ≈ 100 units |
| Overall symbol | ≈ 600–650 units wide/high |

> These are construction guidelines. Optical balance takes priority over rigid mathematical measurements.

### Recommended color hierarchy

- **Deep Blue:** 60–70%
- **Soft Sky:** 20–30%
- **Accent Purple:** 5–10%

Purple should be a signature point rather than a competing primary color. The wordmark should remain a single near-white tone.

## 5. Primary Horizontal Lockup

### Structure

`[ Modular Icon ]    Ekagram`

### Guidelines

- Place the symbol to the left of the wordmark.
- Use a modern rounded geometric sans-serif.
- Use near-white `#F8F9FB` for the wordmark in dark mode.
- Vertically center the wordmark against the optical center of the symbol.
- Use approximately **0.5–0.75× the icon visual height** as the icon-to-wordmark spacing.
- Do not recolor individual letters; the icon carries the color expression.

---

## 6. Icon-Only & App Icon

The icon-only mark is the most important secondary asset. It should be able to stand independently and become the recognizable Ekagram signature.

### Recommended assets

| Asset | Recommended master/export | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Master icon | 1000 × 1000 SVG/vector | Primary source artwork |
| iOS App Store | 1024 × 1024 | No wordmark |
| Android Play Store | 512 × 512 | No wordmark |
| PWA icon | 192 × 192 / 512 × 512 | Web application |
| Social avatar | Square master | Icon only |
| Favicon | 16 / 32 / 48px + ICO | Use simplified construction |

### App-icon rule

The symbol should occupy approximately **60–70% of the canvas**, with enough negative space to remain clean at small sizes.

---

## 7. Responsive / Small-Size System

Do not merely scale the master artwork indefinitely. Small-size variants should be optically redrawn so gaps, tile weight and the E-like negative space remain legible.

| Size/context | Treatment |
|---|---|
| **1000px+** | Full master geometry and detail |
| **512px** | Full icon |
| **128px** | Full icon |
| **64–96px** | Simplified icon if needed |
| **32px** | Simplified but recognizable modular arrangement |
| **16–24px** | Micro mark; remove unnecessary secondary detail |

---

## 8. Wordmark Specification

- **Name:** Ekagram
- **Style:** Bold rounded geometric sans-serif
- **Personality:** Modern, intelligent, approachable and confident
- **Dark-mode color:** `#F8F9FB`
- Avoid decorative modifications and excessive letter spacing.
- The capital **E** should visually relate to the symbol without literally reproducing it.

| Variant | Treatment | Use |

| **Dark-mode primary** | Background `#101923` + full-color icon | Default brand presentation |

> The identity is **dark-mode-first**. Light and monochrome versions are production fallbacks rather than the primary visual presentation.

---

## 10. Clear Space & Minimum Size

Define clear space using the width of one standard modular tile as the base unit **1X**.

Keep at least **1X of empty space** around the complete lockup wherever practical.

### Minimum sizes

| Asset | Recommended minimum |
|---|---:|
| Full digital lockup | ≈ 120px wide |
| Full print lockup | ≈ 25–30mm wide |
| Icon | 16px technical minimum; 32px preferred |
| App icon | Designed and tested at 32 × 32px |

---

## 11. Brand Usage Rules

### Always

- Maintain module proportions and corner radii.
- Preserve the designed negative spaces.
- Use the approved color hierarchy.
- Use the icon-only mark where the display size is too small for the wordmark.
- Keep the logo optically centered.

### Never

- Use gradients.
- Add drop shadows.
- Apply 3D, bevel, gloss or glass effects.
- Randomly rearrange or recolor tiles.
- Distort the proportions.
- Add unnecessary outlines or decorative elements.
- Place the wordmark inside the app icon.
- Use excessive colors at once.

| Context | Suggested treatment |
|---|---|
| **Loading state** | Sequential tile appearance/activation |
| **Notification state** | Subtle emphasis on the purple focal tile |
| **Active state** | Accent Purple `#5B73D7` |

| **Empty states** | Optional outline/simplified mark |
| **Watermark** | Monochrome or low-emphasis single-color mark |

The logo can become part of the product's interaction language without changing its core geometry.

## 14. Final Brand Recommendation

### Recommended core identity

Adopt the **modular-grid symbol** as the official Ekagram core mark. It offers the strongest balance of:

- Community
- Technology
- Organization
- Digital ecosystem
- E recognition
- Scalability

Among the explored directions, the modular-grid approach is the strongest because it avoids relying on generic speech bubbles, people icons or conventional network symbols.

### Color recommendation

Normalize the final production palette to:

```text
Charcoal Navy  #101923
Deep Blue      #355DB7
Soft Sky       #799AE3
Accent Purple  #5B73D7
Near White     #F8F9FB
```

This keeps the identity consistent across the Ekagram product, website, social media, app icons and marketing materials.

### Long-term brand objective

The ultimate success criterion is **standalone recognition**.

The modular symbol should eventually be identifiable as Ekagram even when the wordmark is absent.
